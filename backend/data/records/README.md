# Bundled ECG records

Seven records from the **MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database** (Moody & Mark, 2001), published by PhysioNet under the Open Data
Commons Attribution License v1.0: https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/

Records 100, 105, 200, 210, 213, 222 and 233 are from the inter-patient test split (DS2, de Chazal et al., 2004): the
sender was trained on other patients (DS1), so these are held-out patients for it.

- Moody GB, Mark RG. The impact of the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database. IEEE Eng Med Biol 20(3):45-50 (2001).
- Goldberger AL et al. PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet. Circulation 101(23):e215-e220 (2000).

The data are de-identified research recordings. This project is a research prototype, not a medical device.
